# Local preview and launch requirements

The website is intentionally running without a production domain, real contact details, a CMS account or email credentials. It has no fabricated business history, testimonials, guarantees, qualifications or office address.

Before a public launch, supply and confirm:

- A registered domain and its canonical HTTPS `SITE_URL`.
- A monitored email, a verified phone if desired, and an appointed privacy contact. Replace reserved preview details and turn off `previewContacts` in CMS.
- A Sanity project/dataset and editor access, then verify publishing and image uploads. Optional during local testing.
- A verified Resend sender, recipient and key; distributed rate-limit credentials and salt. Submit a real enquiry and confirm receipt. Exercise provider outage and retry behaviour.
- Actual leasing workflow, marketing and screening arrangements, property access procedures, emergency coordination scope and owner approval thresholds.
- Service scope outside GTA, including on-site availability. The site promises only property-specific confirmation.
- Applicable tax treatment and final fee terms; renewal reminders versus paid lease renewal; move coordination versus inspection charges; annual inspection totals; limits of included Prestige project work versus additional coordination fees.
- Business review of wording about Ontario rent increases, lease administration, property access and LTB administrative coordination. The website does not offer legal representation or guarantee outcomes.
- Privacy policy approval, effective date, retention schedule, access/correction/deletion process, actual provider list and processing locations. The current policy is explicitly a draft.
- Optional real company story, owned photography, business hours and social profiles. Leave absent facts absent until supplied.

Deploy using the README instructions. Keep `ALLOW_LOCAL_TEST_FORMS` unset publicly. Validate headers and the trusted-proxy IP configuration in the actual host. Check the sitemap, canonical URLs, noindex removal and social image against the real domain, then rerun responsive and accessibility checks. Lighthouse targets are not a certification; measure the deployed host and mobile network profile.

No email, enquiry record, external publication, domain purchase or live CMS mutation was authorized or performed during local implementation.
