# Home surface brief
Mode: Persuade. Direction pinned by the user's detailed brief; proceed directly to implementation without another approval round. Use real stock photography rather than generated comps.

Visitor: an owner evaluating who will handle their property. Journey: recognition of place → relief from daily coordination → understand service scope → compare fees → make an informed enquiry. Feeling curve: assured opening, calm explanation, practical services, confidence at pricing, easy final contact. Remembered moment: oversized white Valore editorial heading over Toronto at dusk, framing the owner's city as the investment context.

Grammar: distinct editorial scenes, no continuous flight. Devices: photographic opening; asymmetric image/prose; interactive service index; sequential process; factual comparison. Signature: selecting a service changes the adjacent photographic composition and preserves the service in the quote link.

Layer contract: background Toronto photo drifts at 0.06 scroll speed capped at 32px; neutral luminance overlay is static; semantic foreground text remains stable; white architectural frame resolves into the following light section. On mobile use centred CN Tower crop and smaller translation; reduced motion is static. This is restrained photographic depth per the user's request, not an invented 3D world or composited stock building.

Opening: local context and legible value proposition. Midpoint: photo recedes slightly as owner-oriented copy comes into view. Exit: white frame joins the light introduction without blank scroll space. No generated cutouts or false managed-property imagery.

References: Balinest's readable page demonstrated explicit management paths, photography and a contact endpoint (https://www.balinestvillas.com/). Adapt the clarity, not its copy or claims.
