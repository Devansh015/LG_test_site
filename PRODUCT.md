# Valore Property Group
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, TypeScript and Tailwind, explicitly requested for this new project. Local testing only for now; deployment must remain possible on Vercel.

## Users
GTA and Ontario residential landlords, condo investors, multi-unit owners and out-of-town property owners seeking management or leasing support.

## Product Purpose
Explain the business, compare transparent management packages, and turn qualified owner interest into quote enquiries.

## Positioning
Professional, transparent property management that simplifies ownership and supports long-term property stewardship. Do not invent differentiators, results or social proof.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Seven public pages; shared pricing data; calculator; accessible quote and contact forms with honest delivery states; simple owner-editable CMS. Primary coverage is GTA; confirm services and on-site availability outside GTA individually. Exact Core, Signature, Prestige and add-on prices come from the supplied brief. LTB coordination is administrative only. No fabricated company history or office.

## Brand Commitments
Valore Property Group. Black, red, white. Luxury real estate editorial photography, restrained motion and architectural composition. Confident, concise, approachable language. Primary CTA: Get a Free Quote.

## Evidence on Hand
The user's comprehensive business brief. Empty repository; no supplied logo or photography, testimonials, credentials, contact details or established domain. User confirmed local testing and basic non-identifiable preview contacts. Any architectural stock is illustrative, never a managed-property claim.

## Product Principles
Honest business claims; transparent pricing; accessible owner enquiries; maintainable content; useful motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard operation, reduced motion, small mobile displays. Do not claim certification from automated checks.

## Open Launch Decisions
Verified contacts, domain, notification credentials, service workflow confirmation, tax treatment and final terms, privacy review and CMS project credentials.
